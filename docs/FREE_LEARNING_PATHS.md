# Free AI and computer-engineering learning paths

Researched October 25, 2026 (Asia/Manila). These are recommendations, not completed credentials or enrollment records. No accounts were created, assessments submitted, or certificates claimed.

## Suggested order

1. **Cisco Introduction to IoT and Digital Transformation** — six-hour introduction linking connected hardware, data and automation. A quick starting point for the hardware side of the portfolio. Complete its own required activities/assessment to earn the offered badges.
2. **IBM SkillsBuild Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals** — AI/ML concepts, ethics and applications. A free digital credential requires the prescribed courses, graded quizzes/final assessments and simulations.
3. **Hugging Face MCP course** — practical AI-tool integration, most relevant to the user's developer/AI workflow. The fundamentals certificate requires Unit 1; the completion certificate requires the use-case units. The provider recommends about 3–4 hours per chapter/week. Certificate issuance is free; any external paid API/hosting use is a separate service choice, not an authorized purchase.
4. **Cisco Networking Basics** — 22-hour foundation for networking and connected systems, with Packet Tracer activities. Free student-level badge; issuer lists a 70% comprehensive-exam passing score. This is not CCNA certification.
5. **Elements of AI: Introduction to AI** — an optional deeper conceptual path. Its digital certificate is free; exercise and peer-review requirements must be satisfied. Reviews can delay issuance. The separate Building AI course has a paid certificate, so it is not in this free-certificate shortlist.

## Claiming and publishing a credential

Complete the course and required assessment personally, using the learner's own account and the provider's permitted assistance rules. After the issuer awards the credential, retain its exact title, issuer, issue date, verification URL and, if provided, credential ID/PDF. Publish only the issued credential and its verification URL. Avoid uploading account details or private learner records.

The portfolio has a **Certifications & learning** menu panel. Its verified-certificate area is deliberately empty until issuer evidence is available; the five course links are separately labeled as learning resources, not qualifications held. No issuer logos, award artwork, certificate IDs or completion dates have been fabricated.
